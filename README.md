# ai-journey-agentics

A talk-to-the-org presentation combining pieces of two illustration apps:

- **[alyssas-ai-journey](https://github.com/alyssafuward/alyssas-ai-journey)** — why AI is disruptive (Broad/Deep/Systemic/How to Human/Overview pyramid), the personal journey with AI, and the closing/references slides.
- **[how-to-ai](https://github.com/alyssafuward/how-to-ai)** — the "what is the AI doing" panels: single-agent flow, multi-agent (kitchen brigade), and deterministic vs. probabilistic/guardrails.

## Flow

1. Why AI is disruptive (5 slides)
2. The journey (full hotspot map, growth chart, poems, "Things I've built" — stops before its own closing screens)
3. how-to-ai panels: What is the AI doing → What is the AI doing, together → Deterministic vs. Probabilistic
4. Closing: mom and daughter
5. References & Resources (final slide)
